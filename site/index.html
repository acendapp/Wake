Questions

Good to know

Does the alarm really wake me on silent?

Yes. Wake uses a true system alarm that sounds through silent mode, Do Not Disturb, and Sleep Focus, not a notification you can sleep through. It greets you with a spoken good-morning that rotates day to day.

How is my routine personalized?

Each morning you check in with how ready you feel and what the day asks. Wake builds a short routine to match, and learns from your evening reflections (your energy, mood, and focus) so it keeps getting sharper.

Can I choose the voice that wakes me?

Yes. Pick from several voices, each with its own feel, and Wake rotates through a few recordings so your good-morning never sounds canned.

Is my data private?

Your reflections are yours. We never sell your data or use it for advertising, and you can permanently delete your account and history from inside the app at any time. See our Privacy Policy.

What does it cost?

Wake is free to try for 7 days. After that it's $59.99/year (about $5/mo) or $9.99/month. Cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

Which devices are supported?

Wake is launching on iPhone first. Android is on the roadmap.
